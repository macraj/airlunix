# Licensing and publication status

Upstream components keep their own copyrights, licences and corresponding-source
requirements. A project-level licence cannot grant rights to third-party binaries.
See [the NPU component review](docs/npu-redistribution-review.md) and its
[file inventory](docs/npu-component-inventory.json).

The licence selection for newly authored Airlunix code is awaiting owner
confirmation. No replacement licence is asserted over upstream code. This is a
release preparation blocker, not a reason to remove legal notices.

Public binary publication is held until runtime/model and boot-asset rights,
licence notices and required corresponding-source materials are complete.
The local candidate and private rootfs build snapshot must not be uploaded merely
because the generic image passes a credential scan. No vendor correspondence has
been sent and no permission is inferred from public download availability.
